ABOUT ASHARI JEWELS

Welcome to Ashari Jewels! We are a passionate team dedicated to creating beautiful, gender-neutral jewellery for everyone. Our mission is to provide high-quality pieces that celebrate individuality and self-expression. We prioritize affordability without compromising on craftsmanship, ensuring that our customers can enjoy exquisite jewellery without breaking the bank. Our vision is to become a leading jewellery brand, known for our unique designs and commitment to inclusivity. Our craftsmen and designers work to create pieces that are not only appealing but also of importance to our customer. For they present and reflect our jeweleery and quality through display and purcashing. Every piece in our Ashari Men, Statement Selection which we have designed to fit the needs of our younger audience and Ashari Chic ED collections is designed to be authentic, bold makng a statement where every where you go. With a mix of street for younger audience and class edge for the men and women in suits!

We are commited to creating as much craft as possible through high quality crafts and timeless designs. Whether it be gold,silver,bronze and in between no one is left behind because we believe that there can never be one option in jewellery wear. And so wear it with confidence because we created them with love and designed them with YOU in mind. Whatever you need!

Thank You for choosing Us and for being part of the Ashari family. Be on the look out, our team will be displaying them on pour socials so be sure to follow us there! From the Ashari Jewel Team, we thank you for the love and support and we hope to see you very very soon!

Our mission is to attract people who value quality and affordability in jewellery pieces, furthermore we want to possibly elevate the business through online presence and make jewellery gender-neutral unlike most jewellery brands that only cater and prioritize jewellery pieces for women only. Our vision is to become one of the leading jewellery brands in South Africa and possibly go international and collab with brands such as Pandora's Box and still be able to maintain affordability too!
