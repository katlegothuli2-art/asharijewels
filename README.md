# asharijewels
ST10518741 Katlego Kekana
Project overview:
The organisation’s name is ASHARI JEWELS which specialises in jewellery pieces, ASHARI Jewels was founded in 2022 in Cullinan, Gauteng by a young entrepreneur who had a passion for creating jewellery pieces but then she realised she could do more as she saw how expensive they were and that the longer one wore a jewellery piece the metal material would tarnish, turn the skin green and irritate people because of hf sweat and oils. Furthermore, her main goal was to make jewellery that was comfortable and could be worn for long without causing skin irritation and she analysed that majority of jewellery shops did not cater for men and younger men and also focused on making her own jewellery pieces to be tarnish free, gender-neutral and chic at the same time. 

## Website goals and objections:
Goals
•	To gain exposure which will attract customers and increase online sales.
•	To establish a strong social media presence on Tik Tok and Instagram through marketing and collaboration to drive traffic to our website.
•	To become the go-to brand for affordability, gender neutral and tarnish free jewellery pieces.
•	To encourage customer feedback on our socials after purchase to build trust and positive word of mouth.
Objectives
•	To drive traffic to the website through Instagram, Tik Tok and Facebook business accounts to redirect customers to our website by putting links on our bio.
•	To showcase upcoming pieces and our collection on the website so that customers can browse and purchase.
•	To encourage them to purchase directly through our website. 

# Key features and functionality:
•	Homepage/Index: This page will display the jewellery with the “SHOP NOW” icon for users to purchase. It will introduce visitors to the brand showcase featured products
•	About Us Page: This page will provide background of the business, including our goals and mission for the brand.
•	Products/Services: This page will provide pictures of collections such as Men Collection and Chic ED Collection. It will allow customers to browse through all jewellery items and view product details including prices.
•	Contact Us Page: Provides contact information so customers can reach ius. It includes inquiry forms email address, and phone numbers for customer support and complaints.
